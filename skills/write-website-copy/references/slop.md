# Copy slop (website)

On-demand edit pass for [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). Load on the last
edit before layout/factual verification. These rules apply to **page
copy this skill produces**, not to this skill's own documentation.

Fail the pass if any check below is still true.

## Em dashes

Drafted copy must not contain `—`.

The dash is the fastest AI tell in marketing prose: pause, aside,
punchline, and contrast in one glyph. Heroes that hinge on a dash
usually have nothing under the hinge.

Replace with a period, a comma, a colon, or two sentences. A spaced
hyphen (` - `) used as the same beat does not count as a fix.

| Fail | Pass |
| --- | --- |
| Ship faster — without the busywork | Ship without the busywork |
| Export to CSV — then share with finance | Export to CSV, then share with finance |

Count `—` in the output. The bar is zero, not "fewer." Quoting a source
that already contains `—` is the only exception; keep the quote intact
and do not add more.

## Synonym triplets

Do not pad a claim to three near-synonyms, three stacked adjectives, or
three parallel fragments.

| Fail | Pass |
| --- | --- |
| Fast. Secure. Scalable. | Exports land in Sheets in one click |
| Clarity, consistency, and control | Same filters on every dashboard |
| Build, ship, and scale with confidence | Ship CSV and Sheets exports today |

If you can delete one word and lose nothing, it was padding. Keep the
word you mean. Two real items are fine. Three only when they are
actually three different things the reader must use (CSV, Sheets, and
PDF), and each is an approved fact.

## Laundry lists

Do not dump every benefit into a section because a list looks complete.
Do not auto-structure the page as three bullets per block.

If a list could be a sentence, write the sentence. Bullets earn their
place when the reader must scan or compare distinct items, not when
three slogans are standing in for one claim.

Also cut the cousins that travel with this: "It's not X, it's Y." Bold
lead-in bullets. Title Case headings used as decoration.
("Seamless / intuitive / robust" is the same tell in adjective form.)
